## Selection rules

1. A technology the user specified is binding. Record it as `USER_SPECIFIED`
   and do not re-litigate it.
2. A technology evidenced in `input/references/**` outranks a factory default.
3. An existing codebase's stack outranks a greenfield preference.
4. Otherwise choose the option that is boring, current, and matched to the
   capability profile — and say why.
